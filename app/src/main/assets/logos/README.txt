Optional logo files for the 3D map's brand signs.

Put a PNG here named after the brand id (the first word of its row in the `brands` list in
index.html), for example: tacobell.png, mcdonalds.png, shell.png, kingsoopers.png.
A square image with a transparent background works best. The launcher draws it inside the white
disc on the sign instead of the generic mark. A brand with no file keeps the generic mark.
Logos are trademarks of their owners: use them on your own unit, and leave them out of anything you share.
